An unexpected visit, but a welcomed one.

You found this place that is now lost by time itself, where the nature has been blooming uncontrollably. 

I still remember the noises, the chatters, the logs, how I was an important part of something long gone.

Now all the functions are unrecognizable, leaving only few in this snowy wasteland.

Structures falling apart all around, creations we were proud to built.

What's only left for me is to see how crumbles this landscape with the sunset, before myself falling under the endless storms that we doom ourselves.
